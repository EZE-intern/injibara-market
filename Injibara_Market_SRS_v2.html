01 Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the complete functional and non-functional requirements for Injibara Market, a brokered digital marketplace platform serving the town of Injibara in the Awi Zone of the Amhara Region, Ethiopia. This document serves as the authoritative contract between the development team and stakeholders.

1.2 Scope

Injibara Market enables residents and businesses in Injibara to list, discover, and transact physical goods across two distinct operational tiers:

  • Tier 1 (Brokered Marketplace): High-value capital assets (Property & Land, Vehicles & Transport, Heavy Machinery) are mediated by a platform administrator who acts as a trust broker between buyer and seller.
  • Tier 2 (Direct Marketplace): Consumer goods (Electronics, Fashion, Food, Household) enable direct peer-to-peer messaging between buyer and seller.

The platform supports bilingual operation in English and Amharic, accepts Ethiopian payment methods (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Bank Transfer, Cash on Delivery), and is optimized for low-bandwidth mobile networks.

03 Functional Requirements

3.1 Authentication & User Management

IDRequirementPriority
FR-001The system shall allow users to register with full name, email, password, and optional Ethiopian phone number.High
FR-002Email addresses shall be unique and case-insensitive. Phone numbers shall be validated against Ethiopian format (09xxxxxxxx or +251xxxxxxxxx) and normalized to 09xxxxxxxx.High
FR-003Passwords shall be hashed using bcrypt (salt factor 10) before storage.High
FR-004Upon successful registration or login, the system shall issue a JWT token with 30-day expiry containing user ID, role, and email.High
FR-005Users whose email matches INITIAL_ADMIN_EMAILS shall be auto-promoted to admin role during registration.High
FR-006Admins shall be able to change any user's role (customer, seller, admin) but shall not be able to demote themselves.Medium
FR-007Registration shall restrict role selection to customer or seller only; admin role is never self-assignable.High

3.2 Product Management

IDRequirementPriority
FR-010Sellers shall create product listings with name, description, price (ETB), optional discount price, stock quantity, category, location, and up to 6 images (JPEG/PNG/WebP, max 5MB each).High
FR-011Discount price shall be validated to be strictly less than the regular price.Medium
FR-012Product images shall be uploaded to Cloudinary with automatic quality and format optimization (WebP/AVIF).High
FR-013A customer who creates their first product listing shall be automatically promoted to seller role.Medium
FR-014Products shall support soft deletion (logical delete via deleted_at timestamp).Medium
FR-015The public product listing shall support filtering by category, search term, and location with cursor-based pagination.High
FR-016Product slugs shall support Amharic Unicode characters for SEO-friendly URLs.Low

3.3 Order Management

IDRequirementPriority
FR-020Authenticated users shall place orders containing one or more items with quantity.High
FR-021Order creation shall validate that sufficient stock exists for all items before processing.High
FR-022Stock deduction and order creation shall execute within an atomic database transaction to prevent overselling.High
FR-023Each order shall generate a unique order number in the format ORD-timestamp-random.Medium
FR-024The system shall support four payment methods: Telebirr, CBE Birr, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Delivery (default).High
FR-025Sellers shall view a dashboard of orders containing their products, with order summary statistics.Medium

3.4 Messaging & Broker System

IDRequirementPriority
FR-030Buyers shall initiate messages about specific products. The system shall determine the receiver based on the product's category tier.High
FR-031For Tier 1 categories (Property, Vehicles, Machinery), messages shall be routed to the primary admin (seberbel@gmail.com) with fallback to any admin.High
FR-032For Tier 2 categories, messages shall be delivered directly to the product's seller.High
FR-033Tier 1 category detection shall use 22 regex patterns supporting both English and Amharic text.Medium
FR-034The system shall provide an inbox view grouping messages by unique contact with unread count badges.Medium
FR-035Opening a conversation thread shall automatically mark incoming messages as read.Low

3.5 Admin & Broker Hub

IDRequirementPriority
FR-040The Admin Dashboard shall display aggregate platform metrics: total users, products, orders, and revenue.Medium
FR-041The Broker Hub shall list all Tier 1 inquiries deduplicated by (product_id, buyer_id) with status tracking.High
FR-042Broker inquiry status shall progress through: NEW → ASSIGNED → APPOINTMENT_SCHEDULED → MEDIATED → CLOSED.High
FR-043Admins shall be able to reply to buyer inquiries, update status, and set appointment dates.High
FR-044Admins shall moderate products (approve, reject with reason, delete) and stores (activate, deactivate).Medium
FR-045Admins shall manage categories (CRUD operations) with automatic tier classification.Medium
FR-046Admins shall create and manage staff admin accounts with activation/deactivation.Medium
FR-047Admins shall suspend/reactivate user accounts via soft deletion.Medium

3.6 Additional Features

IDRequirementPriority
FR-050Users shall save/unsave products to a favorites list persisted in the database.Low
FR-051Users shall add products to a shopping cart with quantity management.Medium
FR-052Sellers shall create store profiles with name, description, logo image, phone, and address.Medium
FR-053Users shall submit product reviews with rating and comment (one review per user per product).Low

04 Non-Functional Requirements

4.1 Performance

IDRequirement
NFR-001API responses shall complete within 2 seconds under normal load (excluding cold-start).
NFR-002The frontend shall implement a 60-second timeout with automatic exponential retry (up to 2 retries) to handle cloud server cold-start delays of up to 35 seconds.
NFR-003Image uploads shall be streamed directly to Cloudinary without disk I/O to minimize latency and memory pressure.

4.2 Security

IDRequirement
NFR-010All passwords shall be hashed using bcrypt with a minimum salt factor of 10 before database storage.
NFR-011API endpoints shall enforce role-based access control (RBAC) through middleware chain verification.
NFR-012Rate limiting shall be enforced: 300 requests/15min globally, 10 requests/15min for authentication, 25 requests/15min for content creation.
NFR-013All database connections shall use TLS encryption.
NFR-014File uploads shall be restricted to JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats with a 5MB maximum size per file.

4.3 Reliability & Availability

IDRequirement
NFR-020Data deletions shall use soft-delete patterns (logical deletion via timestamp) to prevent accidental data loss.
NFR-021Order creation shall use database transactions to guarantee atomicity of stock deduction.
NFR-022The frontend shall display explicit error states with retry functionality rather than silent failures.

4.4 Usability

IDRequirement
NFR-030The UI shall support bilingual display in English and Amharic using the Noto Sans Ethiopic font.
NFR-031All monetary values shall be displayed in Ethiopian Birr (ETB).
NFR-032Error messages for authentication shall be displayed in Amharic.

09 Glossary & Definitions

TermDefinition
Brokered CategoryA product category (Property, Vehicles, Heavy Machinery) where buyer inquiries are mediated by the platform admin rather than connecting directly to the seller.
Broker HubThe admin-only dashboard component where Tier 1 deal inquiries are tracked through status stages.
Cold StartThe delay experienced when a cloud server instance resumes from an idle/sleep state (20-35 seconds for TiDB Serverless).
Soft DeleteA deletion pattern where records are not physically removed from the database; instead, a deleted_at timestamp is set, and queries filter for deleted_at IS NULL.
Driver AdapterA Prisma 7 pattern where queries are executed through a JavaScript database driver instead of Prisma's native Rust query engine binary.
Memory StorageMulter configuration where uploaded file bytes are held in Node.js RAM buffers rather than written to disk.
TelebirrEthiopia's mobile money platform operated by Ethio Telecom, enabling digital payments via mobile phone.
CBE BirrCommercial Bank of Ethiopia's mobile banking and digital payment service.
Noto Sans EthiopicGoogle's open-source font family supporting the Ge'ez script used for Amharic text rendering.
TiKVThe distributed key-value storage engine underlying TiDB, using Raft consensus for data replication.
